## Prototypes as shown

Design A was a 420 pt panel: a search field, one 28 pt row per item with a type icon or a 30 × 20 thumbnail, a pin mark on pinned items, ⌘1–⌘9 on the first nine rows, and a footer reading “↩ Paste, ⌘P Pin, ⌘⌫ Delete”. Because the thumbnail was wider than the icons, text started at different positions on image rows; that is the alignment the first comment rejects.

The Settings mock was a single window with: Show panel (shortcut), Launch at login, Show in menu bar, Keep items for (1 hour to 30 days), Maximum size with current usage, Keep text formatting, Skip passwords, Clear History…, and a Paste into apps row shown only while the Accessibility permission is missing.
